Sand Claw

Grab sand, send it around the belt, and match it to the sand artwork to clear the picture before the belt overflows.

  1. Drag to aim the claw. Release to grab.
  2. The sand rides the pipe onto the belt and circles under the artwork.
  3. A bead that matches the sand at the bottom of the artwork sucks it out. Both vanish with a cling, and the sand above falls.

You lose when the belt hits 100% and nothing on it matches the artwork.

Level cleared

The artwork is clear.

Belt overflow

The belt hit 100% and no sand on it matches the artwork.
