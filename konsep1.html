Cat Claw

Grab balls, turn them into cats, and let the cats shoot the artwork away.

  1. Drag to aim the claw. Release to grab a handful of balls.
  2. Balls become cats, one per color, max 10 shots each. New cats go straight onto the belt (max 5 at once).
  3. Cats shoot the first block they face if the color matches. A cat with no target left moves to a parking space, and returns to the belt when it has one again.

You lose when all 5 parking spaces are full and another cat needs one.
