Sand Claw

Free the arrows, ride the beads around the belt, and fill every bin before the belt overflows.

  1. Tap an arrow whose path is clear. It slides off the board.
  2. Each arrow that escapes becomes 10 beads on the belt. A blocked arrow just shakes.
  3. Bins load themselves. When one fills, the bin above it in the same column drops down.

You lose when the belt hits 100% and no loaded bin can take what is on it.

Level cleared

Every bin is full.

Belt overflow

The belt hit 100% and no bin could take the beads on it.
