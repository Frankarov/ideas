Sand Claw

Every level is a pixel-art picture made of sand. Grab it, send it around the belt, and fill every bin before the belt overflows.

  1. Drag to aim the claw. Release to grab.
  2. The sand drops onto the belt and circles the tray.
  3. Bins load themselves. When one fills, the bin behind it in the same column moves up.

You lose when the belt hits 100% and no loaded bin can take what is on it.
